# 360Alert — AI-Powered Safety Monitoring & Alert Management

**360Alert** is an AI-driven safety monitoring and alert management platform designed to help organizations detect potential safety risks, monitor environments in real time, and support faster responses to critical events.

The platform is designed for healthcare facilities, elderly care environments, schools, commercial buildings, industrial sites, campuses, and other locations where proactive safety monitoring is important.

**Website:** https://360alert.io/

---

## About 360Alert

360Alert combines artificial intelligence, cameras, sensors, real-time event processing, and centralized alert management to provide a smarter approach to safety monitoring.

The platform can identify events such as potential falls, unsafe movement, wandering, unusual activity, and other predefined safety conditions. When an event is detected, the system can generate an alert so the appropriate staff or team can respond quickly.

The goal is to help organizations move from passive monitoring toward proactive safety management.

---

## Key Features

### AI-Powered Event Detection

AI technology analyzes movement and environmental activity to identify potential safety risks and unusual events.

### Fall Detection

360Alert can detect potential falls and fall-risk situations and generate alerts for staff members.

### Real-Time Monitoring

Organizations can monitor rooms, devices, and safety events through a centralized dashboard.

### Smart Alerts

Alerts can be categorized and prioritized so teams can focus on situations that require attention.

### Alert Lifecycle Management

Alerts can move through different stages such as:

* Raised
* Accepted
* Resolved

This creates a clearer workflow and improves accountability.

### Centralized Dashboard

The platform provides a single interface for monitoring rooms, devices, alerts, and events.

### Smart Zones

Organizations can define specific areas such as patient rooms, corridors, entrances, or other locations that require monitoring.

### Event Review

Relevant incidents can be reviewed through event information and available video snippets to help teams understand what happened.

### Device Management

360Alert can monitor connected cameras and other supported devices and provide visibility into their operational status.

### Reporting & Analytics

Organizations can review alert patterns, response times, room activity, and other operational information to identify recurring issues and improve safety procedures.

## How 360Alert Works

### 1. Detection

Cameras, sensors, and connected devices provide information about activity within monitored environments.

### 2. AI Analysis

AI-based systems analyze movement and activity to identify predefined safety events.

### 3. Alert

When a relevant event is detected, the platform generates an alert.

### 4. Response

The appropriate staff members can review the alert and take the required action.

### 5. Resolution

Once the situation has been addressed, the alert can be marked as resolved.

### 6. Reporting

Historical alert information can be used to analyze patterns and improve safety operations.
